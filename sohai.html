KNOWLEDGE CORE: GIS & METEOROLOGY

🌧️ The Water Cycle & Rain Formation

Rain is essential to the **Water Cycle**. It involves three key steps:

  • **Evaporation:** Solar heat turns surface water into vapor.
  • **Condensation:** Vapor rises, cools, and forms tiny droplets or ice crystals, creating **clouds**.
  • **Precipitation:** Droplets accumulate and fall to the ground due to gravity (rain, snow, hail).

🌍 Key Meteorological Concepts

Understanding these variables is crucial for risk analysis:

  • **Atmospheric Pressure (hPa):** The force exerted by the air's weight. High pressure brings clear skies; low pressure is associated with storms.
  • **Feels Like Temperature:** The human-perceived temperature, calculated by combining the actual air temperature with **humidity** (Heat Index) and **wind speed** (Wind Chill).
  • **Geographic Information System (GIS):** A system used for capturing, analyzing, and managing location-based data. This application uses GIS ($\text{Lat/Lon}$) to pinpoint weather data on the map.
